# Space Voyage

A 3D space navigation game where you pilot a spacecraft through a safety tunnel back to Earth.

## About

In Space Voyage, you play as a captain returning home after an 8-year space mission. Traveling at 16.18% the speed of light, you must navigate through a series of blue safety gates to avoid invisible space debris. The closer you get to Earth, the stronger the gravitational force becomes, making precise navigation essential. Stray outside the safety tunnel and it's instant death.

## Controls

- **Desktop**: Use keyboard arrow keys to navigate
- **Mobile**: Tap the on-screen directional pads (top, bottom, left, right)

## Graphics Settings

Choose from three graphic quality levels on the welcome screen:
- Low - For older or less powerful devices
- Medium - Balanced performance and visuals
- High - Best visual quality for powerful devices

## Leaderboard

Compete globally! Your score (gates passed, time in game, max gravitational force) is submitted to the Firebase leaderboard. View the top 50 players and try to reach the top.
